# AutoIntel — Ethical Considerations

This document records the ethical commitments behind AutoIntel and how they are
reflected in the product. It is intended for the team, reviewers, and app-store
reviewers, and complements the user-facing
[Safety & Liability Disclaimer](disclaimer.md).

## 1. Safety first

Vehicle diagnostics can affect real-world safety. Our guiding principle is to
**never prioritise convenience over safety**. When in doubt, the app must advise
the user to stop driving and seek professional help rather than encouraging them
to continue. Severity ratings and "safe to drive" language are deliberately
conservative.

## 2. Honesty about AI limits

AI explanations are clearly labelled as AI-generated. We do **not** present AI
output as authoritative or definitive, and we encourage users to verify with a
qualified mechanic. We do not claim that the AI "knows" what is wrong with a
specific vehicle — it only reasons over the code and public reference data.

## 3. Data minimisation & consent

- We collect only the data needed to provide the service (account, vehicles,
  diagnostics, maintenance/fuel).
- We do not sell personal data.
- We are transparent about AI processing of fault-code data (see the
  [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md)).

## 4. Accuracy & correction

We source reference data from open, reputable datasets and attribute them. We
welcome corrections and aim to fix inaccuracies promptly. AI explanations are
generated at request time and cached; users should treat them as advisory.

## 5. Accessibility & inclusivity

Explanations are written in plain English for everyday drivers, not just
technicians. We avoid jargon and define necessary technical terms. Where
possible, we aim to make the app usable regardless of technical background.

## 6. Accountability

We provide clear channels for questions, corrections, and data requests (see the
[Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md) and [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md)).
We will not knowingly provide advice that endangers the user or others.

## 7. Ongoing review

Ethical and safety review is an ongoing responsibility, not a one-time checklist.
We review AI output quality, severity thresholds, and safety wording as the
product evolves, and update these commitments accordingly.
