# AutoIntel — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is AutoIntel?**
An app that connects to a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter, reads fault codes and live
sensor data from your vehicle, and explains them in plain English using AI —
alongside maintenance and fuel tracking.

**Which vehicles are supported?**
Any OBD-II vehicle (most petrol/diesel cars sold since 1996 in the US, 2001 in
the EU) with a compatible ELM327 Bluetooth adapter.

## Accuracy & AI

**Are the explanations always correct?**
No. Explanations are AI-generated or drawn from reference datasets and are
**informational only**. They can be wrong, especially for manufacturer-specific
codes. Always confirm with a qualified mechanic.

**Can I trust the severity rating?**
Treat it as guidance, not a safety guarantee. If a fault affects braking,
steering, airbags, or if you're unsure, stop driving and have the car inspected.

**Why did the AI give a wrong answer?**
AI models can hallucinate or be out of date. We recommend grounding the
explanation with your vehicle's service manual or a professional diagnosis.

## Hardware & Bluetooth

**What adapter do I need?**
A Bluetooth Classic (SPP) ELM327 adapter. Pair it in your phone's Bluetooth
settings before using the app.

**The app can't find my adapter.**
Make sure the adapter is paired, Bluetooth is on, and you've granted the app's
Bluetooth permission. The adapter must be an SPP (classic) type, not BLE-only.

## Accounts & data

**Why do I need an account?**
To keep your vehicles, diagnostics, and history synced to your account across
devices.

**What data do you collect?**
Your account email, the vehicles you add, diagnostic data, and maintenance/fuel
logs. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

**Can I delete my account/data?**
Contact us (see the Privacy Policy) to request deletion.

## Safety

**Should I drive with a fault code?**
Never rely solely on the app. If the check-engine light is **flashing**, stop
driving as soon as it's safe. For any safety concern, have the vehicle inspected.
