## 1. Information we collect

- **Account data:** email address and (optionally) a display name, collected when
  you create an account.
- **Vehicle data:** the vehicles you add — make, model, year, VIN (optional), and
  nickname.
- **Diagnostic data:** fault codes, live sensor readings (e.g. RPM, speed,
  coolant temperature, fuel level), and diagnostic session records read from
  your vehicle.
- **Maintenance & fuel logs:** service events and fuel fill-ups you record.
- **Device data:** basic device/OS information needed to operate the app (e.g.
  for crash reporting), where applicable.

## 2. How we use information

- To provide and personalise the App (e.g. show your vehicles and history).
- To generate AI explanations of fault codes.
- To maintain and improve the App.

## 3. AI processing

When you request an AI explanation, fault-code data is sent to a third-party AI
provider (OpenAI or Google Gemini) to generate the explanation. That provider
processes the data according to its own privacy and data-handling policies.

## 4. Location & Bluetooth

The App uses Bluetooth to communicate with your OBD-II adapter. On Android, this
requires Bluetooth and (on older versions) location permissions. The App does not
collect precise GPS location for the purpose of tracking you.

## 5. Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal information. We may share data with service
providers (AI providers, hosting) strictly to operate the App, and only as
described here. We may disclose data if required by law.

## 6. Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active and as needed to
provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards (encryption in transit, authenticated access) to
protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or
delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to
exercise these rights.

## 9. Children

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from
children.
